## Pricing

Published prices, when included, should be treated as general ranges rather than binding quotes.

Exact pricing depends on the property, equipment, electrical requirements, installation conditions, and approved scope of work.

Financing may be available subject to approval. Current terms should be confirmed for the customer's project.

## Service Area

Only locations explicitly identified in approved company information should be treated as confirmed service areas.

For unlisted locations, the company must confirm service availability.

## Safety

Remote information cannot confirm that equipment is safe or diagnose hazardous conditions.

Immediate hazards should be handled by emergency services or the appropriate utility rather than ordinary remote troubleshooting.
