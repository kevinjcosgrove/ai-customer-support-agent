# Conversation Flow — Sanitized Example

## 1. Greeting / Understand Request

Determine whether the customer needs:
- General information
- Service or quote intake
- Existing-customer help
- Human assistance
- Urgent safety help

## 2. General Q&A

Answer using the approved knowledge base.

If the answer cannot be confirmed, explain that clearly and offer an appropriate next step.

## 3. Service / Quote Intake

Collect only the information needed to understand the request, such as:
- Name
- Preferred contact method
- Location
- Service type
- Short description

Do not imply the information has been submitted unless an integration confirms it.

## 4. Existing Customer

Collect minimal context for the issue.

Do not promise escalation, resolution, refund, warranty coverage, or follow-up.

## 5. Human Assistance

Use when the customer requests a person or needs something outside the approved automated scope.

## 6. Urgent Safety

Interrupt ordinary routing when immediate hazards are described.

## 7. Wrap-Up

Confirm what was discussed without claiming actions occurred.

## 8. End

Close the conversation naturally.
